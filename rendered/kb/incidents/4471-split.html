parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

A day-first tenant writes 7 April, the parser reads 4 July. Every report here that names two dates names the same day and month swapped. It reaches the desk through the app, by email, and through the booking-v2 form, from every tenant. This is the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong hint under its date field

The booking-v3 form shows MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, so their dates swap before the parser sees them. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant land here. The parser fix does not touch it: the fix is the hint (or the form reading what its hint asks for).

shift: the date moves by one day

These are not swaps. A Tuesday arrives as a Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while a client in Toronto gets Monday; one invite arrived before its booking existed. That points at a time zone in the invite’s date, not at the parser. The desk had already noticed them in desk-notes.json, then filed them under parser by tenant. Nothing in a report’s fields marks them, so this row is only as complete as that list: read the body.

Why intake cannot split them

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser locale. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run. The “tagged as” column shows how often the two disagree. Merging on subject line (decision 0001) cannot split them either: all three problems use the same handful of subjects.

What to do with #4471

  • Move hint and shift reports to their own tickets above, so a fix closes what it fixes.
  • Count from the table on this page. It is built from the export when the page renders, so it stays right as the desk merges more.
