parser: the shared parser read every date month first — fixed

A day-first tenant wrote 7 April, the parser read 4 July. Every report here that names two dates names the same day and month swapped, except the one-day moves below. Engineering shipped fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales and closed #4471 on 5 October.

hint: booking-v3 printed the wrong hint under its date field — won’t fix

The booking-v3 form showed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and read the date the tenant’s way, day first. People typed what the hint said, so their dates swapped before the parser saw them. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant land here. The parser fix does not touch it; engineering retired the booking-v3 form instead, so no fix will come.

shift: the date moves by one day — still open

These are not swaps. A Tuesday arrives as a Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while a client in Toronto gets Monday; one invite arrived before its booking existed. That points at a time zone in the invite’s date, not at the parser, so the parser fix does not close it, and retiring booking-v3 does not either: none of these came through a form. The desk listed them in desk-notes.json and counts them under parser by tenant; the table shows them in both places. Nothing in a report’s fields marks them, so this row is only as complete as that list: read the body. If one-day moves keep coming after 5 October, this is why.

Why intake could not split them

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser locale. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run. The “tagged as” column shows how often the two disagree. Merging on subject line (decision 0001) cannot split them either: all three problems use the same handful of subjects.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can hold more than one bug. Closing it on one fix closes the others unfixed.
  • Read the bodies. The cause is in the tenant and the dates, not in the subject or the browser.
